Acme TechCorp — Employee HR Policy Handbook
Version 3.2 — Effective January 2026

1. Leave Policy
All full-time employees of Acme TechCorp are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, in addition to 10 public holidays as per the official company holiday calendar. Annual leave
accrues at a rate of 1.5 days per completed month of service.

Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick
leave exceeding 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward to the next calendar
year.

Maternity leave is provided for 26 weeks as per statutory requirements. Paternity leave is provided for
15 calendar days, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for
planned leave. Emergency leave may be approved retroactively by the reporting manager.

2. Work From Home (WFH) Policy
Employees in eligible roles may work from home up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require Director-level sign-off and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

Employees working from home must remain reachable during core business hours (10:00 AM to 4:00
PM local time) and are expected to maintain a stable internet connection of at least 20 Mbps for video
calls.

The company provides a one-time home-office setup allowance of INR 15,000 for employees who are
approved for a permanent hybrid or remote arrangement.

3. Travel & Reimbursement Policy
Employees traveling for official business purposes are entitled to reimbursement for economy-class
airfare, standard hotel accommodation (up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 per
night in non-metro cities), and local conveyance.

Daily meal allowance during official travel is INR 1,200 per day within India and USD 60 per day for
international travel. All reimbursement claims must be submitted within 15 days of completing travel,
along with original receipts, through the Expense Management System.

Reimbursement for client entertainment expenses requires prior written approval from the employee's
department head and is capped at INR 5,000 per event.

4. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to act with integrity, honesty, and professionalism in all business dealings.
Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to HR immediately upon identification.

Acme TechCorp maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of
any kind. Employees may report violations confidentially through the Ethics Hotline, available 24/7 at
ethics@acmetechcorp.example.

Employees must not accept gifts or gratuities from vendors or clients exceeding INR 2,000 in value
without prior disclosure to their manager.

5. IT & Data Security Policy
All company laptops must have full-disk encryption and an approved endpoint security agent installed
before being used to access company systems. Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and
rotated every 90 days.

Employees are prohibited from storing confidential company data on personal cloud storage accounts
(e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox). All confidential documents must be shared only via the
approved corporate file-sharing platform.

Any suspected security incident, including phishing emails or lost devices, must be reported to the IT
Security team within 2 hours of discovery via security@acmetechcorp.example.

6. Payroll & Benefits
Salaries are disbursed on the last working day of each month via direct bank transfer. Payslips are
made available on the HR portal by the 1st of the following month.

Acme TechCorp provides group health insurance coverage of INR 5,00,000 per employee, extendable
to cover spouse and up to two dependent children at a subsidized premium.

Employees become eligible for the Provident Fund and Gratuity benefits as per applicable statutory
regulations after completing 6 months and 5 years of continuous service, respectively.

An annual performance bonus, ranging from 8% to 20% of base salary depending on individual and
company performance ratings, is paid out in April each year.

7. Probation & Confirmation
New employees undergo a probation period of 6 months from their date of joining. Performance will be
reviewed at the 3-month and 5-month marks. Confirmation of employment is subject to satisfactory
performance and conduct during this period.
